Delete Your Retained Account

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Retained is committed to giving you a clear, working way to request that your account and everything in it be deleted, whether or not you still have the app installed.

The fastest way to delete your account is directly inside the app: open Retained, go to Settings, and tap Delete Account. This deletes everything immediately.

If you no longer have the app installed

You can still request deletion from here. Follow these steps:

  1. Send an email to aliyankhan458@gmail.com from the same email address you used to sign in to Retained.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my Retained account."
  3. We will confirm your identity using the email address you signed in with, and process the deletion within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Requesting deletion, whether through the app or by email, permanently removes your account and all of the following: your email address, every item you logged and its photo, every logged location, your exit rituals, and your push notification information. This cannot be undone.

We do not retain any of this data after deletion. Nothing is kept beyond what is legally required for fraud prevention or security purposes, and Retained does not currently retain anything for those purposes either.
