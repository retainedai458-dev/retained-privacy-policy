Privacy Policy for Retained

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Retained is an app that helps you remember where you left your things and reminds you to check a short list before you leave a place you have marked, such as your home. This page explains what information the app collects, why it collects it, and what you can do about it. We wrote this in plain language on purpose. If anything here is unclear, you can email us at retained.ai458@gmail.com and we will answer.

Who runs this app

Retained is developed and published by an independent developer under the account name Retained. If you ever need to reach us about your data or this policy, retained.ai458@gmail.com is the right address.

Information we collect

Your email address

When you create an account, we ask for your email address. We use it to sign you in through a one time code sent to your inbox. We do not use passwords. We do not use your email for marketing and we do not sell it.

Location information

Retained's whole purpose is remembering where you left things, so location is central to how it works.

When you log an item in the app, we record the precise location of your device at that moment so we can show you where you were when you logged it.

If you set up an exit ritual, such as a reminder to check your door locks and stove before leaving home, the app needs to know when you have left that place. To do this, Retained checks your device's location in the background, even when the app is closed, for as long as you have an active exit ritual turned on. This is the only reason background location is used. It is never used to build a profile of everywhere you go, and it is never sold or shared with advertisers.

While an exit ritual is turned on, Retained shows a permanent notification and checks your location every few seconds as you move, using your phone's location services. This happens on your phone: your movements are not sent to us. We only save that you left a place you marked, and when, so your checklist can open and you can look back at it.

You can turn off background location at any time by removing your exit rituals or by changing the location permission for Retained in your phone's settings. Doing so will stop the exit reminder feature from working, but the rest of the app will still work normally.

Photos

Logging an item includes taking a photo of it, so you can recognize it later and remember what it looks like. That photo is stored, encrypted at rest using industry standard security measures, so you can look back at it whenever you need to. You can delete any item and its photo at any time from within the app.

Map images

When you choose a place for an exit ritual, or look at one you have saved, the app shows a map. The map pictures come from OpenStreetMap, a free public map service run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation. Your device asks OpenStreetMap for those pictures directly, so OpenStreetMap can see your device's internet address and which area of the map is being shown. We do not send OpenStreetMap your name, your email address, your account, or anything you have logged, and we do not control how OpenStreetMap handles these requests. Its own privacy policy is at https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy. The code that draws the map is built into Retained, so no other service is contacted to show it.

Exit reminders and notifications

Exit reminders are created by the app on your phone when it notices you have left a place you marked, and are shown as a notification. They need notification permission, but no notification address needs to be stored on our servers. Earlier versions of Retained did store a notification address and a random identifier so reminders could be sent from our servers; if you used an earlier version, that information is kept until you delete your account, which removes it. The app also uses a random identifier for your installation so it can sign you out on your other phone when you sign in on a new one.

How we use your information

We use the information above only to make the app work the way it is supposed to. Specifically, that means letting you sign in, showing you where and when you logged an item, detecting when you have left a place you marked so we can send you a reminder, and letting you look back at photos you have taken of your things.

We do not use your information for advertising. We do not build advertising profiles. We do not sell your information to anyone, under any circumstances.

Who we share information with

We use trusted cloud infrastructure providers to run our database, secure file storage, and backend logic. Earlier versions of the app also used a push notification service to deliver exit reminders from our servers. These providers process data strictly on our behalf, to make the app function, and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Beyond these service providers, we do not share your information with anyone else, including advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties, and other than the map images described above, we do not have any other integrations that receive your data.

How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account from within the app, your items, photos, logged locations, exit rituals, and push notification information are permanently deleted. This happens right away and cannot be undone, so please be sure before you do it.

Your choices

You are in control of what you share with Retained. Location and camera access are required to log an item, since Retained's core purpose is remembering where and what you left, so declining either means the app cannot log new items for you. Notification permission is optional, though declining it means you will not receive exit reminders. You can delete individual items at any time. You can delete your entire account and all of its data from within the app's settings at any time.

Children's privacy

Retained is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us at retained.ai458@gmail.com and we will remove it.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including access controls on our database that ensure your data can only be read or changed by you. No method of storing or transmitting information is completely secure, but we work to protect your information to a high standard.

Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will let you know inside the app.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy or how your information is handled, email us at retained.ai458@gmail.com.
